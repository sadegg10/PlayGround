HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

  • W A S D — move
  • Mouse — aim camera (click to lock)
  • Left Click / J — Stand strike (hold for combos)
  • Q / E — Stand abilities
  • R — Stand Rush (ultimate)
  • Space — jump · Shift — dash (i-frames)
  • C — summon / dismiss Stand · Tab — swap Stand
  • M — mute · Esc / P — pause

THE RULES

  • Clear every enemy in a wave to advance. A Boss arrives every 5th wave.
  • Dealing damage fills the Rush meter. Full meter means your ultimate is ready.
  • Chain hits to raise your Style Rank (D→C→B→A→AA→AAA). Higher rank = more score.
  • Dash grants brief invulnerability — use it to slip through attacks.
  • Knock enemies into the arena walls for bonus Wall Slam damage.
  • Your Stand fights beside you: press C to manifest it fully.
